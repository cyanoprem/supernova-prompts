## **5. BANKING**

### **Structures (5 structures, 3 sentences each)**

### **Structure 1:**

**"I would like to [Transaction Type] from/into my account."**

- I would like to withdraw money from my account.
- I would like to deposit cash into my account.
- I would like to transfer money from my account.

### **Structure 2:**

**"Can you help me understand this [Document/Statement]?"**

- Can you help me understand this bank statement?
- Can you help me understand this interest rate?
- Can you help me understand this loan document?

### **Structure 3:**

**"I need to update my [Information] in your records."**

- I need to update my phone number in your records.
- I need to update my address in your records.
- I need to update my email ID in your records.

### **Structure 4:**

**"What documents do I need to [Action/Service]?"**

- What documents do I need to open a new account?
- What documents do I need to apply for a loan?
- What documents do I need to update my address?

### **Structure 5:**

**"Is there a fee for [Service/Transaction]?"**

- Is there a fee for online banking?
- Is there a fee for ATM withdrawals?
- Is there a fee for money transfer?
