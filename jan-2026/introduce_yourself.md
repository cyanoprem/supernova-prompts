## **1. INTRODUCE YOURSELF**

### **Structures (5 structures, 3 sentences each)**

### **Structure 1:**

**"My name is [Name]. I am a [Occupation]."**

- My name is Priya. I am a homemaker.
- My name is Shruti. I am a teacher.
- My name is Kavitha. I am a housewife.

### **Structure 2:**

**"I live in [City/Area]."**

- I live in Bangalore.
- I live in Indiranagar.
- I live in Koramangala.

### **Structure 3:**

**"Nice to meet you. What's your name?"**

- Nice to meet you. What's your name?

### **Structure 4:**

**"My husband works in [Field/Company]."**

- My husband works in IT.
- My husband works in banking.
- My husband works in a hospital.

### **Structure 5:**

**"I enjoy [Hobby/Activity] in my free time."**

- I enjoy cooking in my free time.
- I enjoy reading books in my free time.
- I enjoy watching movies in my free time.
