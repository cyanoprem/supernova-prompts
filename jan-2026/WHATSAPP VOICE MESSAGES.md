## **7. WHATSAPP VOICE MESSAGES**

### **Structures (5 structures, 3 sentences each)**

### **Structure 1:**

**"Hi! I wanted to tell you about [Topic/Event]."**

- Hi! I wanted to tell you about the school meeting.
- Hi! I wanted to tell you about tomorrow's function.
- Hi! I wanted to tell you about the new schedule.

### **Structure 2:**

**"Can you please [Action/Request]?"**

- Can you please check the WhatsApp group?
- Can you please send me the homework details?
- Can you please share the photos from yesterday?

### **Structure 3:**

**"I'm sending you [Item/Information] now."**

- I'm sending you the photos now.
- I'm sending you the document now.
- I'm sending you the address now.

### **Structure 4:**

**"Let me know if you [Need/Have] [Something]."**

- Let me know if you need anything.
- Let me know if you have any questions.
- Let me know if you need more details.

### **Structure 5:**

**"Talk to you later. Take care!"**

- Talk to you later. Take care!
