## **6. PARENT-TEACHER MEETING**

### **Structures (5 structures, 3 sentences each)**

### **Structure 1:**

**"How is my child doing in [Subject/Area]?"**

- How is my child doing in mathematics?
- How is my child doing in English?
- How is my child doing in science?

### **Structure 2:**

**"Does he/she participate in [Activity/Class Discussion]?"**

- Does he participate in class discussions?
- Does she participate in sports activities?
- Does he participate in group projects?

### **Structure 3:**

**"What can I do at home to help him/her [Improvement]?"**

- What can I do at home to help her study faster?
- What can I do at home to help him learn better?
- What can I do at home to help her solve problems better?

### **Structure 4:**

**"Is he/she having any problems with [Subject/People]?"**

- Is she having any problems with other students?
- Is he having any problems with mathematics?
- Is she having any problems with science?

### **Structure 5:**

**"Thank you for taking the time to meet with me."**

- Thank you for taking the time to meet with me.
