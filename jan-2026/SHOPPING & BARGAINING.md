## **9. SHOPPING & BARGAINING**

### **Structures (5 structures, 3 sentences each)**

### **Structure 1:**

**"Do you have this in [Size/Color/Type]?"**

- Do you have this in a smaller size?
- Do you have this in blue color?
- Do you have this in cotton material?

### **Structure 2:**

**"How much does this [Item] cost?"**

- How much does this saree cost?
- How much do these vegetables cost?
- How much does this dress cost?

### **Structure 3:**

**"Can you give me a discount on [Item]?"**

- Can you give me a discount on these vegetables?
- Can you give me a discount on this saree?

### **Structure 4:**

**"I'll take [Quantity] of  [Item]."**

- I'll take two kilos of these tomatoes.
- I'll take three pieces of this cloth.
- I'll take five meters of this material.

### **Structure 5:**

**"Do you accept [Payment Method] for payment?"**

- Do you accept UPI for payment?
- Do you accept cards for payment?
- Do you accept online transfer for payment?
