## **8. DOCTOR VISITS**

### **Structures (5 structures, 3 sentences each)**

### **Structure 1:**

**"I've been having [Symptom] for [Number] days."**

- I've been having a headache for three days.
- I've been having a fever for two days.
- I've been having stomach pain for four days.

### **Structure 2:**

**"It hurts when I [Action/Movement]."**

- It hurts when I swallow.
- It hurts when I walk.
- It hurts when I bend down.

### **Structure 3:**

**"Is this medicine safe for [Person/Condition]?"**

- Is this medicine safe for children?
- Is this medicine safe for pregnant women?
- Is this medicine safe for elderly people?

### **Structure 4:**

**"How many times a day should I take [Medicine/Tablet]?"**

- How many times a day should I take this tablet?
- How many times a day should I take this medicine?
- How many times a day should I take this syrup?

### **Structure 5:**

**"When should I come back for a check-up?"**

- When should I come back for a check-up?
