## **10. INTRODUCE FAMILY MEMBERS**

### **Structures (5 structures, 3 sentences each)**

### **Structure 1:**

**"This is my [Relation]. His/Her name is [Name]."**

- This is my husband. His name is Rajesh.
- This is my daughter. Her name is Ananya.
- This is my son. His name is Arjun.

### **Structure 2:**

**"He/She is [Age] years old and works in [Field/Company]."**

- He is 40 years old and works in banking.
- She is 35 years old and works in IT.

### **Structure 3:**

**"My [son/daughter] studies in [Standard/School]."**

- My daughter studies in fifth standard.
- My son studies in St. Joseph's School.
- My daughter studies in third standard.

### **Structure 4:**

**"We have been married for [Number] years."**

- We have been married for 12 years.
- We have been married for 15 years.
- We have been married for 10 years.

### **Structure 5:**

**"My family lives in [City/Place]."**

- My family lives in Mumbai.
- My family lives in Bangalore.
- My family lives in Hyderabad.
