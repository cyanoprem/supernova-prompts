## **2. TALK TO KIDS IN ENGLISH**

### **Structures (5 structures, 3 sentences each)**

### **Structure 1:**

**"It's time to [Activity]. Please [Action] now."**

- It's time to wake up. Please get ready now.
- It's time to eat breakfast. Please come now.
- It's time to sleep. Please brush your teeth now.

### **Structure 2:**

**"Did you [Complete Action] your [Task]?"**

- Did you finish your homework?
- Did you complete your project work?
- Did you pack your school bag?

### **Structure 3:**

**"Good job! I'm proud of you."**

- Good job! I'm proud of you.

### **Structure 4:**

**"Don't [Action]. You need to [Required Action] first."**

- Don't watch TV. You need to study first.
- Don't play games. You need to finish homework first.
- Don't go outside. You need to eat lunch first.

### **Structure 5:**

**"How was your day at school?"**

- How was your day at school?
