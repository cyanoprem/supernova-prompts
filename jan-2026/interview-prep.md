## **4. JOB INTERVIEW PREP**

### **Structures (5 structures, 3 sentences each)**

### **Structure 1:**

**"I have [Number] years of experience in [Field/Role]."**

- I have 5 years of experience in customer service.
- I have 3 years of experience in teaching.
- I have 7 years of experience in administration.

### **Structure 2:**

**"I took a career break to [Reason], but now I'm ready to [Goal]."**

- I took a career break to raise my children, but now I'm ready to return to work.
- I took a career break to care for my family, but now I'm ready to restart my career.

### **Structure 3:**

**"My strengths are [Strength 1] and [Strength 2]."**

- My strengths are communication and time management.
- My strengths are teamwork and problem-solving.
- My strengths are patience and organization.

### **Structure 4:**

**"I'm interested in this position because [Reason]."**

- I'm interested in this position because it matches my skills.
- I'm interested in this position because I enjoy working with people.
- I'm interested in this position because it offers growth opportunities.

### **Structure 5:**

**"When can I expect to hear from you?"**

- When can I expect to hear from you?
