# Supernova English Communication & Soft Skills
## VET College - Complete 360-Day Program Syllabus

---

## Program Overview

**Total Duration:** 360 days (1 academic year)
**Total Concepts:** 60 structured learning modules
**Delivery:** Daily personalized learning activities through the application
**Assessment:** Tests at 90-day intervals (90, 180, 270, 360 days)
**Focus Areas:** Workplace readiness, interview preparation, group discussions, everyday English communication

---

## Phase 1: Foundation Building (Days 1-90)
### 15 Concepts - Grammar & Basic Communication

**Section 1: Foundational Grammar (10 Concepts)**

1. Simple Present Tense
2. Simple Past Tense
3. Simple Future Tense
4. Present Continuous Tense
5. Past Continuous Tense
6. Future Continuous Tense
7. Past Irregular Tense
8. Articles (a, an, the)
9. Prepositions of Time
10. Prepositions of Place

**Section 2: Personal Communication Skills (5 Concepts)**

11. Introduce Yourself
12. Introduce Your Family and Friends
13. Talk about your Educational Background
14. Describing People and Places
15. Talking about Hobbies and Interests

**Phase 1 Assessment:** 90-Day Test covering all foundational grammar and basic communication skills

---

## Phase 2: Professional Readiness (Days 91-180)
### 15 Concepts - Group Discussions & Interview Preparation

**Personal Communication Skills - Advanced (3 Concepts)**

16. Sharing Personal Information Clearly
17. Talk about Strengths and Weaknesses (Strength → Benefit → Weakness → Action)
18. Talking about Daily Routines

**Group Discussion Skills - Core Techniques (7 Concepts)**

19. Expressing Opinion
20. Agreeing with Others
21. Disagreeing Politely
22. Adding to Someone's Point
23. Asking for Clarification
24. Giving Examples
25. Concluding a Discussion

**Interview Preparation Basics (5 Concepts)**

26. Basic Greeting and Meeting Etiquette
27. "Tell me about yourself" - Professional Introduction
28. Answer Interview Questions
29. Answer Resume Questions
30. Discussing Education and Qualifications

**Phase 2 Assessment:** 180-Day Test covering Group Discussion skills, Advanced Personal Communication, and Interview Preparation basics

**Final Assessment:** 360-Day Comprehensive Evaluation of all 60 concepts

---

## Learning Methodology

Each concept is taught through:

- **Structured Lessons** with clear examples and explanations
- **Practice Exercises** using real-life scenarios
- **Interactive Activities** delivered daily through the application
- **Progressive Difficulty** building systematically from basic to advanced
- **Continuous Feedback** to track individual student progress

## Expected Learning Outcomes

### By End of Phase 1 (90 Days)
✓ Master fundamental English grammar tenses
✓ Introduce self, family, and friends clearly
✓ Discuss educational background confidently
✓ Describe people, places, and hobbies accurately

### By End of Phase 2 (180 Days)
✓ Express opinions confidently in group discussions
✓ Agree, disagree, and add points politely in professional settings
✓ Present themselves professionally with proper greeting etiquette
✓ Answer "Tell me about yourself" and common interview questions
✓ Discuss educational background and strengths/weaknesses effectively

### By End of Phase 3 (270 Days)
✓ Participate effectively in topic-based group discussions
✓ Handle advanced interview questions with confidence
✓ Write professional emails and follow-up communications
✓ Present career goals clearly
✓ Demonstrate appropriate body language and presentation skills

### By End of Phase 4 (360 Days)
✓ Communicate fluently in workplace environments
✓ Understand and respond to instructions accurately
✓ Make polite requests and ask for permissions appropriately
✓ Engage in workplace small talk and social conversations
✓ Use correct grammar and tenses in all daily communications
✓ Make recommendations and decisions in group settings
✓ Handle everyday situations with English fluency
