# Group Discussion Guide - Simple Sentence Structures

---

## 1. Expressing Opinion

**Base:**
- "I think **[topic]** is very important because **[reason]**."
- "In my opinion, **[topic]** is better because **[reason]**."

**Optional Extension:**
- "Many people use it every day."
- "It helps save time."

## 2. Agreeing

**Base:**
- "Yes, I agree with that."
- "Exactly. **[topic]** is really **[adjective]**."
- "He/She is right about **[topic]**."

**Optional Extension:**
- "It is very useful."
- "Many people think the same."

## 3. Disagreeing Politely

**Base:**
- "Maybe, but **[topic]** has problems."
- "I'm not sure about that. It is difficult."
- "What about **[topic]**?"

**Optional Extension:**
- "Some people don't like it."
- "It costs too much money."

## 4. Adding to Someone's Point

**Base:**
- "Also, we should think about **[topic]**."
- "Yes, and **[topic]** is also important."
- "Another point is **[topic]**."

**Optional Extension:**
- "People use it daily."
- "It saves time."

## 5. Asking for Clarification

**Base:**
- "Can you explain that?"
- "What do you mean?"
- "Sorry, I didn't understand. Can you say that again?"

## 6. Giving Examples

**Base:**
- "For example, in **[place]**, people use this."
- "Like **[topic]**, right?"
- "For example, **[topic]** is very **[adjective]**."

**Optional Extension:**
- "Many students do this."
- "It is very common now."

## 7. Concluding

**Base:**
- "So **[topic]** is the best option."
- "In conclusion, **[topic]** is very important."
- "I think **[topic]** is better because **[reason]**."

**Optional Extension:**
- "This is good for everyone."
- "Most people agree with this."

---

## Ultra-Safe Reason Banks

Use these ready-made reasons when you need support:

**Why something is good:**
- "because it saves time"
- "because it is easy"
- "because people use it daily"
- "because it is cheap"
- "because it is safe"
- "because it helps students"
- "because it is convenient"

**Why something has problems:**
- "because it costs too much"
- "because it is difficult"
- "because it takes too much time"
- "because it is not safe"
- "because not everyone can use it"

---

## Word Banks

**Standardized Placeholders:**
- **[topic]** - the main subject (online shopping, social media, etc.)
- **[reason]** - why something is good/bad
- **[example]** - a real-life instance
- **[place]** - location or setting
- **[adjective]** - describing word

**Common Topics:**
online shopping, social media, work from home, online learning, internships, mobile phones, technology, education, health, environment

**Safe Adjectives:**
important, useful, expensive, cheap, difficult, easy, dangerous, safe, good, bad, convenient, common

**Places:**
my country, India, cities, villages, schools, offices, homes, online, shops

**Connecting Words:**
however, also, but, and, because, so, for example, in my opinion
